Our story · स्वाद

A table where everyone finds their flavour

Aroma is a multi-cuisine family restaurant on the 3rd floor of Basant Central in Sakchi — a warm, wood-and-gold dining room made for long lunches, birthday dinners and easy get-togethers.

The menu travels widely: North Indian and Mughlai classics, Indo-Chinese favourites, continental bites, and the biryani and kebabs guests mention most — with vegetarian and non-vegetarian choices for the whole table.

  • Veg & non-vegSomething for every guest at the table.
  • Family diningComfortable seating and room for groups.
  • CelebrationsBirthdays, anniversaries and group dinners.
A typical meal for two: approx. ₹500–₹800 for two
